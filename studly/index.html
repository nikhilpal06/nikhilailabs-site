How Studly works

From confusion to confidence in three steps.

1

Capture the problem

Take a photo, upload an image, or type your question. Before it answers, Studly shows you what it read — so a misread digit gets caught before it becomes a wrong answer.

2

Learn the method

Studly explains the solution step by step and highlights the formulas, rules, and concepts in play — and where the maths can be checked, it verifies the answer with a symbolic engine.

3

Practise and master

Try a similar question, show your own working for feedback, and watch each topic move toward "strong" as the evidence builds.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Studly?
Studly is an AI-powered mathematics learning app. It gives step-by-step explanations, personalised practice, solution verification, and honest progress tracking — so you understand the method, not just the answer.
Does Studly only give the final answer?
No. Studly is designed to explain the reasoning and concepts behind a solution so you can solve similar problems on your own. You choose how much help you want — a hint, a guided walkthrough, the full solution, or the concept taught first.
What math levels does Studly support?
Studly is built for learners from middle school through college, across topics from arithmetic and algebra to trigonometry, calculus, and statistics. It adapts the explanation to your level rather than aligning to one specific curriculum.
Can Studly check my own solution?
Yes. Photograph the steps you wrote yourself and Studly reads them, checks each one, and tells you which step went wrong — not just that the final answer was.
How does verification work?
Where the mathematics can be checked, Studly re-computes the answer independently with a symbolic maths engine — not by asking the same AI a second time. The reasoning steps are explained, not formally proved, and when something can't be verified, Studly says so rather than sounding confident anyway.
Is Studly free?
Yes — the free plan includes 5 AI Assists a day with two follow-up questions each. Studly Plus raises that to 20 a day, routes your answers to a stronger model, and adds solution verification and personalised practice. Current pricing is shown on the in-app paywall.
Is Studly a replacement for a teacher?
No. Studly is a learning companion designed to support classroom instruction, independent study, and practice — not to replace a teacher.
How do I sign in, and how is my data handled?
No passwords — sign in with Apple, Google, or a one-time email code. Studly is for learners aged 13 and over. Photos of your work are deleted automatically after 90 days, and problems sent for solving carry no name or email. See the privacy policy for details.
Will Studly support subjects beyond math?
Mathematics is the initial focus. Additional study tools and subjects may be introduced in future updates.
